## Full Description - EN

Pratiqo brings teacher search, lesson requests, and learning tools into a simple mobile experience.

Students and parents can explore profiles, find a teacher, request a lesson, and follow their bookings. Teachers have a space to manage their profile, availability, and requests.

YoPratiqo complements the experience with free interactive intros: flashcards, quizzes, audio, and cultural context to help learners prepare before real practice with a teacher.

What you can do:
- Search for a teacher on Pratiqo
- View teacher profiles and lesson formats
- Request online or in-person lessons where available
- Track bookings in your account
- Explore free YoPratiqo intros
- Access help, safety, and policy information

Pratiqo does not promise academic outcomes, exam success, immigration results, employment, teacher income, ranking, or booking volume. Features may vary by country, profile, and availability.
