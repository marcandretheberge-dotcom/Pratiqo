# YoPratiqo first-mission release check

The automated suite starts with fresh browser storage, uses the real onboarding
and authored first mission, and verifies that a pause and reload restore the
learner's place. Listening prompts tested through their transcript are assisted
practice. An automated browser passing is not iPhone or native-accent approval.

## Physical iPhone

On the exact candidate deployment, record the iPhone model, iOS version, browser,
language, deployment URL, and the first step that fails. Use a fresh Safari
profile for each of Italian, English, Québec French, Spanish, German, Brazilian
Portuguese, Japanese and Mandarin. Do not reset a real learner's account.

1. Start from the public Languages tab as a beginning learner. Confirm that
   Yoppo is visible and visibly reacts while choosing an interest. Check the
   smallest supported width, with no overflowing text or obscured controls.
2. Follow the visible recommendation without coaching. Verify that the learner
   can recognize the next action, skip the arrival and mission films, and reach
   real target-language content. With Reduce Motion enabled the film must not
   block the lesson or start unexpectedly.
3. Use an available recorded prompt, then try the clearly labelled transcript
   fallback. The transcript must not be treated as independent listening.
   With a foreground/background interruption, check that sound does not keep
   playing over the next screen.
4. Leave partway through the mission, close Safari, reopen the same route and
   resume. Compare the heading, question/cursor, and progress before and after.
   Finish the mission, confirm the celebration can be dismissed, and verify
   that resuming opens the next mission, not a duplicate reward or reset.
5. Report a row per language: arrival, first task, audio/fallback, saved return,
   finish and next mission. Record pass/fail for each, rather than inferring
   eight-language coverage from the reference course.

This check does not certify curriculum depth, account sync across devices, live
speech, paid Passes, continued offline access, or App Store readiness.
