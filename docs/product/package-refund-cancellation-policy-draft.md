# Package refund & cancellation policy — draft

**Status:** Product / legal **draft**.  
**Requires legal review before public launch.**  
**Not legal advice. Not final Terms.**

Related: [`package-product-decision.md`](./package-product-decision.md), [`paid-packages-legal-launch-checklist.md`](./paid-packages-legal-launch-checklist.md).

---

## Principles (draft)

1. Packages are **proposed by independent teachers**; Pratiqo facilitates payment and tracking.
2. Teacher payout is **progressive** after completed sessions — unused value should not be treated as already paid to the teacher.
3. Credits (Phase 2) are **tied to one teacher**; not generic Pratiqo money.
4. **No** automatic expiration of package value as a growth tactic without legal approval.
5. Transfer of a package to **another teacher** is not allowed unless explicitly approved in a later product decision.

---

## Scenarios (draft — counsel must finalize)

### Before any session occurs

- Student may request cancellation under rules to be set in Terms (timing windows TBD with legal).
- Prefer full applicable refund of unused package when no session was completed and no teacher transfer occurred.
- Admin/manual review path for edge cases.

### After some sessions occur

- Completed sessions generally remain charged (subject to teacher no-show / quality policies TBD).
- Unused / untransferred sessions may be refunded per approved calculator.
- Partial refunds must reconcile with transfers already made.

### Unused sessions

- Remain on the package balance until used, cancelled, or refunded per policy.
- Do not silently expire for marketing pressure.

### Teacher cancellation

- Student should be offered reschedule, unused-session handling, or refund of applicable unused balance (policy TBD).
- Further teacher transfers for cancelled undelivered sessions must stop.

### Teacher no-show

- Session not treated as completed for payout.
- Student remedy: reschedule and/or applicable refund (TBD with legal).

### Student no-show

- May count as completed or forfeited under teacher preference / platform rules — **must be clarified in Terms** before launch.
- Must not create ambiguous double charging.

### Package transfer to another teacher

- **Not allowed** in Phase 1 / Phase 2 unless a later decision explicitly allows it with legal review.

### Manual / admin review

- Dispute, chargeback, suspected abuse, teacher disappearance, calculator edge cases.
- Admin tooling required before public launch (see checklist Phase 2).
