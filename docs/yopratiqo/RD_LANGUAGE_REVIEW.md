## Learner-Facing Treatment

- Page 13 keeps three sections: general Spanish, forms the learner may hear,
  and safe beginner production.
- Regional forms are for recognition first. The learner is not asked to imitate
  them.
- Variation warning remains: Dominican speech varies by person, region, and
  situation.
- Final /s/ weakening is treated as pronunciation awareness. The workbook keeps
  the normal spelling `dos cafés`.
- `ahorita` is treated as context-dependent nearby time. The practical beginner
  strategy remains `¿A qué hora, por favor?`
