## Scope

This workbook covers Module 3: eating, ordering, and paying in the Dominican Republic. It is a print-friendly workbook rendered through the existing YoPratiqo print shell. Learners use browser print / Save as PDF; the route does not pretend to offer a downloadable PDF.

It is intentionally separate from Module 2. It reuses the workbook architecture pattern only: generated answer references, derived response counts, solutions isolated at the end, and print page breaks. It does not copy Module 2 scenarios, artifacts, answer sequence, or prompts.

## Workbook Shape

- 22 logical pages.
- About 160-220 derived learner response opportunities.
- Support progression: guided, reduced, independent, assessment, solutions.
- Answer pages are isolated at the end:
  - objective key;
  - model responses and teacher extension.
- The menu, bill, prices, and receipt data are fictional pedagogical values.
- The listening bridge points to the existing digital Module 3 route and does not create new audio infrastructure.

## Pedagogical Mechanics

The workbook uses recognition, menu scanning, matching, controlled form practice, retrieval ladder, constraint solving, order building, listening bridge, payment lab, writing, speaking prep, cumulative retrieval, performance check, self-assessment, and derived answer keys.

The final performance check uses four domains:

- COMPRENDO: understand a new short menu.
- RECUERDO: retrieve key phrases without notes.
- PRODUZCO: produce a short order/payment exchange.
- RESUELVO: solve unavailable/fast-speech situations.

## Safety Notes

- No real restaurant names are used.
- No current price, tax, or service-charge norm is asserted.
- Allergy phrases are accompanied by a safety note: learners must confirm with staff and not rely on worksheets.
- Cultural content is framed as recognition and respectful questions, not broad claims about all Dominicans.
- Teacher extension content is at the end and does not share raw quiz answers or progress.
