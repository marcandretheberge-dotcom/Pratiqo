## Anti-Stereotype Guard

The workbook avoids claims such as:

- “all Dominicans...”;
- “Dominicans always...”;
- unsupported “national dish” claims;
- real restaurant or current price assertions;
- service/tip/tax norms as universal facts.

The workbook wording prefers:

- “you may see/hear”;
- “to recognize”;
- “ask respectfully”;
- “fictional practice value”;
- “confirm with staff.”
