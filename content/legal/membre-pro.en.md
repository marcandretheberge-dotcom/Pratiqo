## 3. Nature of Pro Member

Pro Member is an **optional paid membership** — a **member status** offered to eligible teachers on Pratiqo.

The Pro Member status may give access to **economic or platform advantages**, including, **where offered**:

- a **reduced commission** on marketplace lessons, at the rates and on the conditions displayed by Pratiqo;
- a **rebate, reward, or cashback structure** linked to the teacher's activity, on the conditions displayed;
- **subscription benefits** (e.g., paid features attached to the account) where offered;
- possible **visibility or marketplace advantages** where offered by the platform.

The exact set of advantages, their **terms**, **availability**, **rates**, and **caps** may **vary by plan, region, time, and current offer**, and are disclosed **on the platform, in the dashboard, at checkout, on the subscription page, or in the applicable plan details**. Pro Member advantages **may evolve** without any fixed list of advantages being guaranteed in perpetuity.

Pro Member is **not** a tooling suite or a teacher studio: it is a member status. Any distinct feature that may be tied to the status, where offered, is described separately on the platform.

## 15. Relationship with payouts, commissions, and rebates

The **Pro Member subscription fees** (what Pratiqo charges for the **member status**) are **distinct** from **marketplace commissions** (the percentage Pratiqo retains on each lesson), service fees, and other marketplace fees.

The **annual Pro Member profit-sharing / rebate**, where offered, is **conditional**. It corresponds to **5% of eligible Pratiqo marketplace net profits, excluding profits generated by YoPratiqo and any other separate self-paced product**, is planned for **January**, is capped by a maximum pool of **CAD $150,000**, activates only if at least **10 eligible Pro teachers** qualify, and each teacher can receive a maximum of **CAD $10,000** according to their **relative annual volume**. Pratiqo does not guarantee any minimum income or any profit-sharing amount.

- Paying for Pro Member **does not exempt** the teacher from marketplace rules, commissions, cancellation rules, refund rules, or chargeback obligations.
- Where a **reduced commission** is tied to Pro Member status, its rate, conditions, effective date, and scope are disclosed on the platform, in the dashboard, at checkout, on the subscription page, or in the applicable plan details. Pratiqo may adjust this rate **prospectively**, subject to applicable law.
- Where a **rebate, reward, or cashback structure** is tied to Pro Member status, its **calculation method, caps, payout frequency, and clawback conditions** are disclosed in the same way. A rebate already paid out may be **adjusted, clawed back, or offset** by Pratiqo, to the extent permitted by law, in case of refund, chargeback, error, ineligibility, or breach of these terms.
- **Pro Member fees** cannot be offset against marketplace payouts, unless Pratiqo expressly offers that feature or applicable law requires it.
