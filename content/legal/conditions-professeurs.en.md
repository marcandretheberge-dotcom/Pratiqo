These Teacher Terms should be read together with the **[Terms of Service](/legal/cgu)**, **[Privacy Policy](/legal/confidentialite)**, **[Cancellation Policy](/legal/annulation)**, and **[Code of Conduct](/legal/code-de-conduite)**.

## 2. Scope of these Teacher Terms

These Teacher Terms apply to any person who offers, advertises, organizes, or delivers teaching, tutoring, mentoring, coaching, or training services through Pratiqo.

In this document, **“teacher”** also includes tutors, instructors, mentors, and other learning service providers using Pratiqo.

## 3. Independent contractor status

The teacher acts as an **independent service provider**. The teacher is not an employee, agent, partner, franchisee, representative, or joint venturer of Cours Pratiqo Inc.

- Pratiqo is a marketplace and technology intermediary.
- The teacher controls their own teaching methods, availability, pricing where the platform allows it, work organization, and tax obligations.
- Nothing in these Teacher Terms creates an employment, partnership, agency, franchise, or joint venture relationship between the teacher and Cours Pratiqo Inc.
- The teacher remains responsible for their equipment, teaching environment, and legal compliance.

## 4. No guarantee of income, students, bookings, ranking, or visibility

Pratiqo does not guarantee:

- students;
- booking volume;
- minimum or recurring income;
- search ranking;
- profile views;
- conversion rates;
- business, teaching, or financial results.

A profile's visibility may vary based on demand, location, availability, relevance, profile quality, reviews, responsiveness, safety policies, and other factors that may change over time.

## 5. Teacher profile accuracy

The teacher must provide accurate, current, and non-misleading information.

This includes:

- their real name or accurate professional identity;
- disciplines, levels, skills, experience, and methods;
- prices, lesson formats, availability, and service areas;
- teaching locations and online or at-home options;
- diplomas, certifications, licences, or affiliations when displayed.

Displayed diplomas, certifications, and professional titles must be genuine. Pratiqo may request supporting documents. Self-declared experience must not be presented as verified by Pratiqo unless Pratiqo has expressly verified it.

## 6. Credentials, verification, and trust-signal disclaimer

Signals displayed on Pratiqo have distinct meanings:

- **Identity verified**: Pratiqo or a verification provider has confirmed certain identity elements.
- **Diploma verified**: a specific diploma or credential has been reviewed under the applicable process.
- **Self-declared experience**: the teacher provided the information, but it is not necessarily verified.
- **Profile completeness**: certain profile information has been completed.
- **Paid plan or Pro Member**: this is a product or commercial status (e.g., the Pro Member membership), not a teaching-quality certification.

These signals do not guarantee teaching quality, learning outcomes, absolute safety, future teacher availability, or the absence of complaints.

## 7. Teacher obligations

The teacher agrees to:

- deliver lessons professionally, prepared, and on time;
- respect confirmed schedules and communicate promptly if an issue arises;
- use Pratiqo messaging where required by the platform;
- protect student and parent personal information;
- maintain a safe, appropriate, and lawful teaching environment;
- comply with the **[Code of Conduct](/legal/code-de-conduite)** and **[Cancellation Policy](/legal/annulation)**;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, sexual, intimidating, abusive, or inappropriate conduct;
- not provide unsafe, illegal, or non-educational services through Pratiqo.

---

## 8. Minors and home lessons

Teachers must exercise extra care when a student is a minor.

- Parent or legal guardian involvement or authorization may be required.
- For minors, a parent or guardian should be present or reachable during the lesson, depending on the student's age, context, and location.
- Unnecessary, ambiguous, or inappropriate private communications with minors are prohibited.
- Lessons must take place in safe, appropriate, and lawful settings.
- The teacher may refuse a location or situation that they reasonably consider unsafe, inappropriate, or non-compliant.
- Any incident or concern involving student safety must be reported promptly to [`support@pratiqo.ca`](mailto:support@pratiqo.ca). In an emergency, contact emergency services first.

---

## 9. Communication and off-platform conduct

The teacher must not attempt to bypass Pratiqo.

Prohibited conduct includes:

- moving a booking or payment off-platform to avoid Pratiqo fees, commissions, or rules;
- soliciting direct payment before, during, or after an introduction created by Pratiqo;
- unnecessarily sharing personal contact details before a confirmed booking;
- encouraging a student or parent to avoid Pratiqo's payment, messaging, or booking tools.

Pratiqo may suspend, restrict, or remove an account for circumvention or attempted circumvention.

Teacher conduct may affect a student's refund eligibility, a payout hold, or the opening of a resolution case. No-shows, repeated lateness, late cancellations, misrepresentations, or lesson-delivery issues may lead to enforcement action.

## 11. Payouts, holds, reversals, and chargebacks

Teacher payouts may be delayed, held, adjusted, reversed, or offset in cases involving:

- a refund or refund request;
- a dispute, chargeback, or bank contestation;
- suspected fraud, abuse, or circumvention;
- an identity, tax, or regulatory compliance issue;
- a safety complaint, student complaint, or internal investigation;
- violation of these Teacher Terms, the Terms of Service, or the Code of Conduct;
- a lesson not delivered, substantially non-conforming, or treated as a teacher no-show.

Stripe, bank, and payment-provider timelines may affect when a payout or refund appears.

Where permitted by law and platform rules, Pratiqo may recover from future payouts amounts linked to a no-show, misconduct, misrepresentation, fraud, policy breach, chargeback, or failed lesson delivery attributable to the teacher.

## 14. Confidentiality and privacy

The teacher must protect student and parent personal information.

Without authorization, the teacher must not publish or share:

- screenshots of conversations;
- audio or video recordings;

- school, family, medical, or financial information;
- any information identifying a student or parent outside what is necessary for the lesson.
