**Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** May 2026

> ⚠️ **Important**: This document supplements the Pratiqo Terms of Service and applies only to YoPratiqo self-paced courses (one-time purchases). It does not replace the Terms. Where a mandatory statutory warranty conflicts with anything below, the statutory warranty prevails.

## 1. What you are buying

A YoPratiqo purchase grants you a **personal, non-transferable licence** to access the content of the purchased module. It is **not a sale of the content itself**.

- Access is **bound to your Pratiqo account**. Account sharing, resale, and content redistribution are prohibited.
- **Cours Pratiqo Inc.** retains all intellectual property rights in the content, visuals, audio, code, and pedagogy of the module.
- No permanent download of the content is guaranteed — playback is delivered through the YoPratiqo platform.

YoPratiqo is an **educational preparation** product. Modules may help you practise, discover a language, or prepare for a conversation with a teacher, but Pratiqo does not guarantee fluency, exam success, an immigration result, an employment result, or a booking with a teacher. The refund policy applicable to paid digital modules remains subject to legal review before any broader paid rollout.

## 2. "Lifetime access" — defined

**"Lifetime access"** means that your access to the purchased module remains active **for as long as Cours Pratiqo Inc. operates the YoPratiqo service**.

### 2.1 Continued operation

While YoPratiqo is operated by Pratiqo, you keep access to any module you have paid for, with no recurring fees.

### 2.2 Discontinuation of a module or of the service

If Pratiqo decides to discontinue a specific module, or the YoPratiqo product line as a whole:

- Pratiqo will notify affected buyers by email **at least 90 calendar days before** the effective shutdown date.
- Buyers who paid for the affected module **within the 12 months preceding the announcement** are entitled to a **pro-rata refund** of the unused period, calculated linearly over 12 months.
- Beyond 12 months from the purchase, no refund is owed, but during the notice window you can **export your progress data** (completed lessons, scores, flashcards reviewed).
- Pro-rata refunds are issued in the original purchase currency, to the original payment method.

## 3. 30-day satisfaction guarantee

### 3.1 Conditions

- **Window**: 30 **calendar days** from the purchase date shown on the Stripe receipt. If day 30 falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Quebec public holiday, the deadline rolls to the next business day.
- **Eligibility**: you must have completed **25% or fewer** of the lessons in the purchased module. Progress is measured by the ratio `lessons completed / total lessons in the module`. Time spent, quiz attempts, and flashcards reviewed are **not** factored into this calculation.
- **Limit**: one satisfaction-guarantee request per buyer **and** per module.

### 3.2 How to request a refund

- From the platform: **My account → My payments and receipts → Request a refund**.
- Or by email to [`support@pratiqo.ca`](mailto:support@pratiqo.ca) with your `order number` and the reason.

### 3.3 Decision and timing

- The refund is issued **via Stripe to the original payment method**.
- Time to appear on the statement: **5 to 10 business days** after issuance.
- **Taxes (GST, QST)** charged on the original purchase are included in the refunded amount.
- **Multi-currency**: the refund is issued in the **original purchase currency**, not in the current display currency on your account. Foreign-exchange differences do not entitle you to an adjustment.

## 4. EU right of withdrawal (14 days)

This section applies to buyers residing in the **European Union**, regardless of where Pratiqo is established.

- YoPratiqo content is **digital content not supplied on a tangible medium**, delivered immediately upon payment.
- At checkout, an EU buyer must **expressly consent** to immediate delivery **and acknowledge** that this consent entails the **waiver of the 14-day right of withdrawal** provided by **Directive 2011/83/EU (art. 16(m))** and **Directive (EU) 2019/770 (art. 16)**.
- **Without that express waiver**, the EU buyer keeps the **full 14-day right of withdrawal** and is entitled to a **full refund on request** within that window, **even if more than 25% of lessons have been completed**.
- The 30-day commercial guarantee in **section 3** applies **in parallel** to EU buyers who waived their withdrawal right.

## 5. Minors

### 5.1 Quebec

- Under **article 157 of the Civil Code of Québec**, a **minor under 14 years of age** cannot enter into a consumer contract alone. Any YoPratiqo purchase by a buyer declaring to be under 14 **requires prior authorization from a parent or legal guardian**.
- Under **article 163 of the Civil Code of Québec**, **a parent or legal guardian may request rescission** of a purchase made by their minor child without authorization. The **refund is then full** and is not subject to the conditions of section 3 (30-day window, 25% cap).
- Minors **aged 14 to 17** may contract for needs **ordinary and usual to their age** (art. 157). The parent or guardian may still seek rescission if the contract causes **prejudice** to the minor (art. 163).

### 5.2 Outside Quebec

For buyers residing outside Quebec, **local minor-protection rules apply** (English Canadian common law, EU consumer law, etc.). Pratiqo cooperates in good faith with any rescission request supported by reasonable evidence of minority and lack of parental authorization.

## 6. Exclusions

The 30-day satisfaction guarantee is **not granted** in the following cases:

- **Repeated abuse** of the guarantee across linked accounts (same card details, same IP address, same device, same primary email address).
- Accounts **closed for fraud**, Terms violation, or chargeback abuse.
- Requests filed **more than 30 days after purchase**, unless the EU provision in section 4 or the minors provision in section 5 applies.
