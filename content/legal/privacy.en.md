# Privacy Policy — Pratiqo

**Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** April 2026

> ⚠️ **Important**: This document is a preliminary template. Have it reviewed by a data protection lawyer before launch.

---

## 1. Introduction

Cours Pratiqo Inc. (“Pratiqo”, “we”) is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what your rights are.

This policy complies with the **Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)** and **Quebec Law 25** (An Act to modernize legislative provisions as regards the protection of personal information).

---

## 2. Data Collected

### 2.1 Data provided by the user

- First and last name, email address
- Profile photo (optional)
- Address (for at-home lessons)
- Payment information (processed by Stripe — not stored by Pratiqo)
- Teacher profile description, disciplines taught, rates
- Progress notes and homework (teachers)

### 2.2 Automatically collected data

- IP address and browsing data
- Device type and operating system
- Geolocation (with your explicit consent)
- Platform usage data

### 2.3 Third-party data

- Login information via Google (name, email, profile photo)

---

## 3. Use of Data

We use your data to:

- Create and manage your account
- Facilitate teacher-student connections
- Process payments and disbursements
- Send booking notifications and lesson reminders
- Improve the platform and personalize the experience
- Meet our legal obligations

We **never sell** your personal data to third parties.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

- **Stripe**: payment processing
- **Supabase**: database hosting (servers in Canada/USA)
- **Google**: authentication (if you use Google sign-in)
- **Vercel**: platform hosting
- Competent authorities if required by law

---

## 5. Geolocation

The geolocation feature is used to display nearby teachers. Your location is only collected with your **explicit consent** and is not stored permanently.

---

## 6. Cookies

Pratiqo uses cookies to:

- Maintain your logged-in session
- Remember your preferences
- Analyze platform usage (Google Analytics)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, which may affect some features.

---

## 7. Data Retention

Your data is retained:

- For as long as your account is active
- For **3 years** after account closure (legal and tax obligations)
- Transaction data is retained for **7 years** (Canadian tax obligation)

---

## 8. Your Rights (Quebec Law 25 / PIPEDA)

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data
- **Correct** any inaccurate information
- **Delete** your data (right to erasure)
- **Data portability** in a structured format
- **Withdraw your consent** at any time
- **File a complaint** with the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI) of Quebec

To exercise these rights, contact us at: [privacy@pratiqo.com](mailto:privacy@pratiqo.com)

We will respond within **30 days**.

---

## 9. Security

We apply security measures including:

- SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions
- Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
- Role-based data access control (RLS — Row Level Security)
- Continuous access monitoring

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within **72 hours** in accordance with Law 25.

---

## 10. Minors

Pratiqo is not intended for children under 13. If a minor uses the platform, parental consent is required. If you believe a minor has created an account without parental consent, contact us immediately.

---

## 11. Modifications

We will notify you of any substantial modification by email with **30 days** notice.
