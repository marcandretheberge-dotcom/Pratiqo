# Code of Conduct — Pratiqo

**Version:** 1.0 · **Last updated:** May 2026

> ⚠️ **Important**: This document is a minimum framework that applies to anyone using Pratiqo (students, teachers, parents, and legal guardians). A more detailed version will be published later. Where mandatory law conflicts with anything below, the law prevails.

---

## 1. Respect and appropriate conduct

Every interaction on Pratiqo must remain **respectful, professional, and consistent with a learning environment**.

- Language used in messaging, reviews, and comments must stay courteous.
- Sexual, violent, degrading, or hateful remarks are prohibited.
- Content that is illegal under Canadian or Quebec law is prohibited.

---

## 2. Safe communication

Pratiqo's in-app messaging is the official communication channel between students and teachers.

- Keep conversations on the platform until a pedagogical relationship is established.
- **Never share sensitive information** by email or SMS that is not strictly required for the lesson (social insurance number, credit-card numbers, passwords, etc.).
- Report any inappropriate message via the **"Report"** button in the conversation thread, or by email to [`support@pratiqo.ca`](mailto:support@pratiqo.ca).

---

## 3. Minors and home lessons

The safety of minors is a priority.

- For any student under the age of majority, **a parent or legal guardian** must authorize the lesson and is encouraged to be present or reachable during home lessons.
- A teacher must **never request to be alone** with a minor in a private space without parental approval.
- Home lessons must take place in an **open or readily accessible area** of the home (living room, study room, etc.), not in a closed bedroom.
- Any concern about the safety of a minor must be reported immediately to [`support@pratiqo.ca`](mailto:support@pratiqo.ca). In an emergency, contact emergency services first (911).

---

## 4. No harassment or discrimination

Pratiqo enforces a **zero-tolerance** policy against harassment and discrimination, online and in person.

The following are prohibited:

- Discriminatory remarks or conduct based on origin, colour, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, civil status, or any other ground protected by the **Quebec Charter of Human Rights and Freedoms**.
- Psychological or sexual harassment in any form.
- Unsolicited advances or contact.
- Sharing intimate content without explicit consent.

---

## 5. No off-platform payment solicitation

All transactions related to a lesson booked through Pratiqo must be processed through the platform.

- It is **strictly prohibited** to ask a student to pay outside Pratiqo (Interac e-Transfer, cash, bank transfer, other apps).
- This rule protects the student (receipt, recourse in case of dispute) and the teacher (proof of lesson, tax tracking).
- Soliciting off-platform payment may result in **immediate suspension** of the account, with no notice and no refund of subscription fees.

---

## 6. Reporting and enforcement

Any breach of this code may be reported to Pratiqo.

- **How to report**: by email to [`support@pratiqo.ca`](mailto:support@pratiqo.ca), or via the **"Report"** button on the relevant profile or conversation thread.
- **Confidentiality**: the identity of the reporter is handled confidentially, unless legal disclosure is required.
- **Possible measures**: warning, temporary suspension, permanent account closure, and, in serious cases, referral to the competent authorities.
- The Terms of Service (`/legal/cgu`) and Cancellation Policy (`/legal/annulation`) remain the contractual reference for administrative consequences.
